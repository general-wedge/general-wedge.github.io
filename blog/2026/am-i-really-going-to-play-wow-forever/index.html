Am I really going to ply WoW...forever?

October 18, 2026 #musings #gaming #opinion

Yes. Yes, I will.

I’m fully invested, I even bought the Collector’s Edition (to my partners dismay - it wasn’t cheap). This is a return to my childhood, it’s nostalgia in video game form, its the escapism that I’ve loved for most of my life. My inner nerd is beaming.

I am having a bit of an identity crisis though, do I move over to Forever and forget Retail? Do I play both? I think I have to choose.

I’ve played WoW now for roughly 20 years - bit depressing saying out loud; I have no regrets - but the game is currently split between multiple versions. You have the theme park style game that is dubbed “Retail”, this is the modern version of the game where 20 years of my progression and achievements live, including characters I’ve had for that amount of time, I spend majority of my play time here. Then there is “Classic” which is the remake or ode to the 2004 version of the game, many of the quality of life changes that are in modern don’t exist here and are viewed with extreme prejudice by the player base (they don’t want anything to change), but it is more an MMO, more exploration and adventure, a world you can get lost in.

With WoW Forever, there is a promise of a return to the original “Classic” game but with new content, updated graphics, and some quality of life changes. By the way, the lighting changes are absolutely stunning. Well done, Blizzard, well done.

So we now have three versions of the game, the split widens even more. My issue now is where do I spend my time? I’m a new Dad, and my time is precious and splitting it between Retail and Forever is quite the commitment, one in which I don’t think I can honour. While I enjoy the modern game, the promise of Forever really speaks to me. The adventure, escapism, a huge open world to explore, where spontaneous encounters are the life blood and magic of the game is what I’d like to spend my limited time doing, not checking off a list of tasks, season after season. Not to mention the seasonal content is just a reskin of a previous season content, over and over, ad nauseum.

We’ll have to see how this promise delivers on November 4th, but this millenial is eagerly awaits with optimistic anticipation. Until then, my friends, zug zug.
